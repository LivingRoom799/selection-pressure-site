Privacy

Selection Pressure uploader. Last updated 6 November 2026.

What this is

The Selection Pressure uploader is a private command-line tool. Its only user is the owner of the Selection Pressure YouTube channel. It uploads that channel's own videos and checks their status. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google data it uses

  • youtube.upload: to upload the channel's videos, as private or scheduled.
  • youtube.readonly: to confirm which channel is signed in, and to read back an uploaded video's status.

It doesn't read, collect, store or share data about any other person, channel or viewer.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file only that account can read. It is never sent anywhere except to Google to make the requests above. There are no analytics, cookies, ads or third-party services.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the token file on the computer also ends access.

The uploader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions go through the contact details on the channel's About page.
