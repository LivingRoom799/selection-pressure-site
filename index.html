Selection Pressure

Tiny creatures. One problem. Forty generations to solve it.

Selection Pressure is a YouTube channel of evolution simulations. Every video is a real, seeded run: creatures with genes like speed, size and eyesight, one pressure on them (a shrinking pond, a predator, a long winter), and mutations that sometimes win. Nothing is scripted. The same seed always replays the same run.

The narration explains why things happen: what each gene costs, why one body type takes over, and what caused a crash. Every frame is drawn by our own code from the simulation data. No image-generator art.

The uploader
